# Predeclared finite primary-actual observer-freeze replay

12 October 2026. This protocol is written before generating this study's outcomes.
It does not change historical Stage 3 sources, results, gates or the immutable
observed-state observer. Root review of the source-bound preflight receipt is
required before generation. Unfavorable outcomes are retained and end this step.

The experiment contains one 3 s practice trial and two separate 3 s output-OFF
assessments for each of four arms, four synthetic learning-law alternatives and
two body grids: 32 practice trials and 64 assessments. Subject seed 2, initial
coefficient scale 1.0, original cue, physiology and learning constants are fixed.
The grids are 2.5 ms and 1.25 ms; measurement/decision spacing stays 5 ms. The
same actual 600-by-2 sensor-noise array is shared across all arms and both grids
within each family/session. Twelve predeclared noise IDs are 1261012100 through
1261012111, assigned family-major, then practice/retention/transfer. Their
exclusion from prior bound noise identities is checked before generation.

The arms are observe-only, known learner goal, expert external reference and
expert neural-derived reference. All use the identical frozen common observer
prior, whole accepted external-position prefix, synthetic 60 ms movement prior,
state capacity, measured calibration exposure and processing schedule. The
expert library is equally available; observe-only and learner-goal arms do not
use it to choose a target. The expert neural-derived representation is offline;
there is no qualified live learner spinal-neural increment in this replay.

Admission uses the per-horizon maximum allocation over all sixteen development
drift records and all thirty-two fresh-confirmation grid records. Each allocation
is already maximum observed coordinate residual plus the unchanged 3 mm label
allowance. Their common exact-prefix current-support domain is 0.100..2.845 s on
the 5 ms grid, with empty action history and known 1 Hz instruction. All 26
horizons, 25..150 ms, must stay within 15 mm. The calibration temporal holdouts
8.100..11.845 s are separate diagnostics and grant no startup authority. No
covariance is substituted for an empirical bound. No model is refitted.

During practice, causal observer updates consume only arrived observations.
Warmup decisions at 0.200..0.800 s every 25 ms commit the far 150 ms baseline target.
At decision 0.975 s the latest support is 0.950 s. Freeze all 26 predictions for
physical targets 0.975..1.100 s and permanently seal the observer for that trial.
Every arm performs this same computation. No later measurement revises the
observer, decision or forecast. Actual position under actuation would be factual
total position, never a voluntary-state measurement.

At 1.000 s process the already available support 0.975 s measurement before resolving
the one-shot launch permit. This is a separate delayed diagnostic because the
historical guard requires strictly future physical targets. Commit the other 25
targets 0.980..1.100 to the independent delayed-residual guard. Preserve missing,
stale, replayed, nonfinite and expired-target rejection. A diagnostic failure
latches a veto independently of the historical guard. A permit is resolved once,
then remains spent/withheld; no second frame or replacement choice is allowed.
Forecast authority ends at physical support 1.100 s. The last measurement normally
arrives 1.125 s; its missing-target deadline is 1.150 s. A clean empty guard never
creates new launch authority.

Only the inherited actual electrical trace domain is studied: dose fraction
1.01318359375 +/-0.4411205888605442. This is an earlier measured-trace reuse,
not a guarantee of future dose. Every inherited isolated response domain fails
under this interval. Record that gate separately from the unavailable learning-
enabled intervention-response interaction allocation: the response kernel's
calibration/validation disabled learning. Both are independent blockers. Do not
silently substitute the unearned 4% monitor target or the 7.4% nominal assumption.

For all six independently tested signed route patterns, record prospective
response means, full gain/dose product intervals and exact-phase independent
residual allocations over the whole frozen target vector, even when the candidate
is unavailable. Add the candidate response once to the zero-command baseline.
The admission interval includes teacher-reference error and must be within 25 mm;
the total-position residual interval excludes teacher error. The two historically
untested mixed patterns stay unavailable. Zero wins ties. The primary gates are
expected to withhold every nonzero action; do not force a favorable intervention.

An unexpected nonzero authorization is a software regression: persist its
observed prefix and decision, then halt **before** model delivery. No active
delivery path is implemented or claimed by this finite actual-only replay. Its
empty actual-monitor and pending-response ledgers follow from zero launch; they
do not qualify monitoring. Separate software probes check the existing API's
completed-monitor availability, full gain/dose product and rejection of an
unqualified response release after 600 ms. Those probes supply no active-driver,
monitor or physical shutdown evidence. A future active integration would need
completed actual monitors on their real clock, independent body-only true charge,
live pending-response uncertainty and post-delivery veto without undoing a pulse.

Practice permits learning under each declared synthetic alternative. Each
assessment resets position/activation/delay, applies the same 2% forgetting to
the practice-final coefficients, disables learning and hard-disables output.
Assessments are 3 s at known 1 Hz and heldout 1.15 Hz. Their metrics are separate from
practice. Complete reset/washout is a simulator premise, not physical evidence.

Save complete sanitized observations, frozen/warmup commitments, diagnostic and
guard witnesses, candidate gates, permit resolution, monitor/pending ledgers and
independent evaluator truth. Keep actual noise arrays and private positions/theta
only in evaluator fields. Segment raw files by family/grid; deterministic gzip
has zero timestamp. Save all counts, failures, matched-arm differences, grid
differences and OFF metrics. Source/input hashes and exact dependency versions
are bound before generation. Default verification audits saved observations and
replays the measured-input controller; optional reproduction regenerates the
same frozen trials, which is not another independent experiment.

Scoped negative controls cover actual-domain versus learning-interaction gates,
empty-action/support/clock qualification, observer sealing/future poisoning,
prelaunch diagnostic cancellation, immutable permit replay, missing-target and
expiry rejection, independent monitor timing and unqualified tail release. They
are software contract probes, not biological or electronic qualifications.

The stop rule is this one finite primary-actual replay plus verification and
interpretation. All-withheld and zero retained advantage are valid results.
No additional sensitivity sweep, four-trial intervention sequence, human skill
transfer, learning multiplier, universal body automation, target firmware or
qualified full implant is claimed.
