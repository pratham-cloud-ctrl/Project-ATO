# Project ATO: engineering roadmap and Stage 3 closeout

13 October 2026. The conservative Stage 3 investigation and conditional concept
v1 are complete within their separate scopes; detailed
electronics, firmware, populated CAD and final project integration remain. This
plan preserves scientific gates and negative results and does not declare the
full project complete.

## Intended result

Project ATO develops a proposed invasive spinal cord–computer interface through
supported theory, mathematical mechanisms, editable electronics, target software,
populated parametric CAD and an illustrated research paper in a private GitHub
repository. AstroSam's Mind-Link is credited as the inspiration for translating a
future interface into an inspectable engineering project.

The causal chain remains **implanted spinal contacts → acquisition →
interpretation → learner-specific stimulation → observed response → feedback**.
The two applications are expert-informed practice with assistance-OFF retention
assessment, and feedback for a selected physiological function. The current
physiological model concerns pressure support. Neither a general body-control
capability nor transfer of an expert's memories or voltages is assumed.

The complete deliverable is digital. Biological observability, selective
recruitment, plasticity, chronic packaging and healthy-consumer usefulness remain
explicit future evidence requirements. Those questions cannot be completed by
adding more software tests. Video and website work remain parked.

## Why the stage remained open

Connecting the complete loop exposed dependencies that isolated demonstrations
did not resolve. The earlier expert generator failed a task check, baseline
delivery uncertainty was too large, and independent changing-learner recordings
exceeded the forecast allocation. New monitors and revised references address
specific causes; they do not silently rescue earlier failed experiments.

The diagnostic found severe noise amplification in unconstrained local harmonic
inversion; it did not prove that every 75 ms predictor must fail. A structured
observer with a measured calibration prior has now completed a
[standalone confirmation](../experiments/stage3/observed-state-observer/README.md):
all 16 fresh sensor-noise pairs on both body grids remain within 15 mm, with a
maximum allocation of 11.354 mm. Persistent memory, a known synthetic movement
prior and calibration are additional resources. This verifies a finite no-dose
desktop domain only; the original negative pilot remains unchanged.

Verification also exposed a platform-dependent near-zero argmax witness. Its
reproduction fix independently binds both witnesses to physical apertures while
preserving the numerical tolerances, all outcomes and all admission decisions.

## Finite Stage 3 closeout

Complete one bounded predictor revision and its connected interpretation:

**Closeout, 12 October:** all four finite steps are complete, as reviewed in the
[exit ledger](stage3/exit-review.md) and [integrated verdict](stage3/stage3-verdict.md).
The new actual-only replay retains 32 practice and 64 OFF sessions, 32 sealed
vectors, 32 pre-launch diagnostics and 1,600 passing guard comparisons. Every
tested nonzero candidate rejects; this is a negative active-assistance verdict.
Fifteen contract probes, independent raw interval/data recomputation and full
same-cohort reproduction pass. No active delivery path or post-dose monitoring
was newly validated by the zero-launch replay. The list below records the
completed closeout contract rather than assigning more Stage 3 work.

1. **Freeze one observed-state method.** Use measured calibration only, known
   instructions and an explicit structural movement prior. Declare state,
   covariance, coefficients, calibration exposure, history, initialization,
   reset and missing-data rules. Private learner parameters and future achieved
   motion cannot select or update the runtime predictor.
2. **Separate development from confirmation.** Existing inspected recordings
   remain development data. Freeze the procedure and settings before generating
   an independent confirmation cohort. Retain fixed comparators, all eligible
   physical supports, cue reversals, all 26 execution horizons and rejection
   cases. Keep the 15 mm forecast, 3 mm label and 25 mm complete-correction
   allocations unchanged. Typical covariance is not an empirical maximum bound.
3. **Resolve the connected pilot.** Consume the observer in the
   [reviewed bounded reset-trial integration](stage3/observer-integration-plan.md), with matched resources,
   live delivery uncertainty, timed cancellation and OFF assessment. Compare
   actual characterized delivery separately from prospective monitor precision.
   Preserve unavailable references and zero/harmful/dependent plasticity cases.
   The first replay has one practice trial; repeated intervention-modified
   sessions need further state-domain evidence. Learning-enabled response
   interaction is not supplied by the inherited learning-OFF calibration.
   Retain any forecast, delivery, interaction or correction failure and withdraw
   unsupported active-demonstration claims; do not tune thresholds to force pulses.
4. **Issue the Stage 3 verdict and report.** Consolidate both application
   mechanisms, the verified operating domain, negative controls, numerical
   sensitivity and the electronics handoff. State which digital claims are
   supported, which conditional capabilities remain hypotheses and what future
   evidence could overturn the conclusions.

Completion means this selected digital investigation has an audited conclusion
and a concrete hardware/software contract. It does not require a favorable
learning result or resolution of every biological uncertainty. A failed
confirmation remains a valid engineering result, while the proposed consumer
application stays explicitly conditional. Further model revisions become
separately justified extensions rather than an unbounded Stage 3 checklist.

## Stages 4 and 5

The [conditional concept revision](stage3/conditional-concept.md) reconnects this
handoff to the user's intended future-interface approach. Fourteen primary
research anchors support a fifteen-entry premise ledger. A separate 128-scenario
illustrative model demonstrates causal assistance and positive/equal/adverse
assumed learning, with no unique neural-information advantage. Its finite scope
does not require qualification of the earlier actual-delivery branch. Further
general observer/audit families are not prerequisites to native circuit/CAD work.

Stage 4 has already begun with the [native dual-source monitor](../electronics/stage4/monitoring-cell/README.md)
and [33-run behavioral circuit study](../electronics/stage4/monitoring-study/README.md).
Their 4% phase-charge target remains unearned. A
[reviewed first driver boundary](stage4/current-driver-design.md) now has a
[156-component native implementation](../electronics/stage4/current-driver/README.md)
with five reviewed sheets and a [bounded circuit/permission study](../electronics/stage4/driver-study/README.md).
This is a restricted 24 V/3 mA/200 µs slice, with external supervision, actual
delivery-path limits and two retained out-of-compliance partial traces. Now
the [native recording slice](../electronics/stage4/recording-front-end/README.md)
adds sixteen shared-reference differential streams and isolated input switching
across seven sheets. Its [finite input/protocol studies](../electronics/stage4/recording-study/README.md)
retain weak-signal/fault failures and revise SPI timing/power explicitly.
Next come full contact routing and qualified shutdown supervision,
power/reference/telemetry and the target firmware backend. Reconcile actual
component power, timing, buses and memory before a complete PCB.

Stage 5 uses the resulting board and component identities to build the native
parametric assembly: populated boards, package, ports/feedthroughs, lead
interfaces, clearances, drawings and STEP exports. Existing allocation solids
are starting constraints, not a finished populated implant.

The final stage joins the mechanisms, circuit/firmware evidence and CAD into the
illustrated manuscript and a reproducible private repository release. Each
digital result retains its limits and contribution record.

## Proposed additional tools

These are recommendations checked against primary product documentation on
12 October 2026, not installation or compatibility claims.

| Tool | Recommendation and intended purpose |
|---|---|
| Ansys Student | Useful optional thermal/structural corroboration for a defined package/lead problem. Existing installation is partly verified; solver/license checks and the two missing original-media archives must be resolved before reliance. Keep exportable CAD in FreeCAD/build123d or Fusion. [Official capabilities and limits](https://ansys.synopsys.com/academic/students/ansys-student) |
| Ansys Electronics Desktop Student | Useful selected low-frequency field, parasitic, thermal or telemetry studies using Maxwell/Q3D/Icepak/HFSS as appropriate. Student restrictions include mesh caps, no geometry export, Circuit's 50-component limit and restricted netlist/command-line workflows. It does not replace KiCad or the ngspice current-loop study. [Official limits](https://ansys.synopsys.com/academic/students/ansys-electronics-desktop-student) |
| Autodesk Fusion Education | A reasonable optional native CAD authoring/review interface if preferred. Eligible students have renewable educational access. Existing FreeCAD/build123d can already produce editable engineering geometry; changing CAD tools is not a prerequisite. [Education access](https://www.autodesk.com/education/edu-software/fusion) |
| MecAgent | Postpone a Fusion-dependent decision. The current official page identifies SolidWorks as available and shows Fusion among forthcoming integrations; the education page offers a limited Free plan, not an established unlimited Fusion workflow. [Current integrations](https://mecagent.com/), [education](https://mecagent.com/education) |
| KiCad MCP | Worth a small disposable-project compatibility trial before using it on authoritative files. This is a category of third-party implementations, not one official KiCad product. A candidate advertises KiCad 10 schematic/board tools; verify actual placement, connection, native reopen/ERC/DRC and export behavior locally. Keep the native files, CLI checks and independent pin audit authoritative. [Candidate implementation](https://github.com/ProductOfAmerica/mcp-server-kicad) |

An additional interface is useful when it demonstrably improves a concrete
design or review operation. It does not establish the biological assumptions or
remove the need to check the generated electronics and geometry.
