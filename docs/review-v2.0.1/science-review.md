## Finding and bounded correction

The reference register listed Ruijia Wang twice consecutively, and its formatted
author string repeated `Wang R`. This propagated to the v2.0.0 manuscript's final
reference page. The original publisher's
[author list for Kathe et al., 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05385-7)
was reopened during this review and contains one Ruijia Wang. The linked
[author full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9668750/) was also
opened. One duplicate full-name entry and one duplicate abbreviated entry were
removed from [the reference register](../stage6/references.json); the corrected
record has 26 authors. The study's result, cohort, DOI and inference boundaries
are unchanged. Regeneration and visual review of the corrected manuscript are
part of the integrated release review, not asserted by this metadata edit.

The [Phillips et al., 2025 primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12443590/)
was also reopened as a targeted check on the pressure mechanism. This pass did
not systematically retrieve all references again. The broader source access
depth and evidence verification date remain recorded in the existing
[evidence review](../stage6/evidence-review.md).

The project retains both central application families and the AstroSam-inspired
progression from a future concept to inspectable theory, native electronics,
firmware, CAD and a research paper. It credits Mind-Link as the inspiration and
does not claim to have invented spinal interfaces. Website and video production
remain outside this review.

| Defensible undemonstrated assumptions permitted | Biological information, alignment, selective authority and adaptation premises carry consequences and failure cases | Aligned; prior demonstration of the complete conjunction is not imposed as a digital completion gate |

Hybrid cortical-input precedents remain labelled hybrid, and clinical restoration
is not silently equated with healthy enhancement. The additional consumer task
families remain a broader conditional vision rather than implemented whole-skill
transfer. No significant conceptual mismatch requiring an overhaul was found.

## Accepted dependencies and closure

The current target does not acquire live spinal feedback during MOTOR; the host
replay uses a disclosed synthetic companion adapter. Analog precision, physical
timing, chronic signal quality, useful expert information, healthy retained
benefit, miniaturization and compact thermal closure remain future dependencies.
They are already disclosed and do not invalidate the finite digital concept.

This pass found no additional claim, mathematical or consumer-mechanism defect
requiring a conceptual change. Its closure is bounded: correct the identified
bibliography defect, rebuild and inspect affected publication outputs, preserve
the explicit assumptions and retain the negative results.
